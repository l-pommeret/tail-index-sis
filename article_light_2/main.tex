\title{Tail-Index Sure Independence Screening for\\High-Dimensional Heavy-Tailed Regression}
\author{Anonymous manuscript}\date{}
\begin{document}\maketitle
\begin{abstract} We propose a marginal screening procedure for identifying covariates that determine a positive conditional tail index in high-dimensional heavy-tailed regression. The method exploits a projection principle: conditioning on a single covariate produces a mixture whose tail index is the upper envelope of the underlying conditional tail-index surface. This yields an exact characterization of marginal detectability and motivates ranking covariates by their average projected tail index. We estimate these scores using empirical-rank local Hill profiles and establish uniform concentration over $p$ coordinates, leading to sure screening and exact recovery when $\log(pn)=o(n\alpha h\Delta_{\min}^2)$. A rank-aggregated version improves stability with respect to the parameters. Simulations show that the proposed screen can distinguish tail-index activity from finite-quantile scale effects, and an application to violent-crime rates illustrates its use. 
\end{abstract}
