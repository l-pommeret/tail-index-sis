## Theory
- Primitive assumptions named (C1) conditional regular variation and
  (C2) uniform tail regularity; full fibre support is a separate condition
  (S), used only where needed. The essential-supremum identity is stated
  without (S); the fibre-maximum identity uses it.
- Detectability proposition restated without measure-theoretic shorthand:
  Δ_j > 0 iff I_ε \ π_j(M) has positive Lebesgue measure.
- Estimation assumptions reorganized into three conditions in the spirit of
  Gardes–Podgorny (H.3–H.5), adapted to diverging p, empirical-rank windows
  and deterministic counts (their density conditions H.1–H.2 are not
  needed):
  (E1) continuous conditional cdf (strict monotonicity dropped;
       generalized quantiles suffice) + uniform equicontinuity ω_ξ of the
       projected indices;
  (E2) normalized slowly-varying remainder o(Δ_min) beyond the working
       tail level (keeps logarithmic factors such as ℓ(s) ≍ (log s)^{-2});
  (E3) uniform local log-quantile regularity over |u−v| ≤ C₀h with
       truncation τ_n = (pn)^{-2}.
- Main results restated in terms of n, p, h, α, Δ_min only:
  score concentration max_j |Ψ̂_j − Ψ_j| = O_P(√(log(pn)/(nαh))) + o(Δ_min)
  under log p = o(nh²) and log(pn) = o(nαh); sure screening under
  log(pn) = o(nαh Δ_min²); exact recovery split into a corollary.
  The finite-sample uniform profile bound (with auxiliary η, t, τ, K_n,
  S_n, b_n, ω_n) moved to the appendix.
- Proofs rewritten line by line for the weakened (E1): the PIT
  representation Y = Q(1/V, U) is established under continuity alone.
  All other ingredients preserved: simultaneous DKW rank control,
  deterministic window counts, 4n+1 window states, conditional PIT + Rényi
  spacings, three-term Hill decomposition, deterministic separation step.
- Verification classes for (E1)–(E3) collected in an appendix: finite
  mixtures with a spectral gap (polynomial modulus), plateau-maximizer
  arrays with dependence (modulus {log n}^{-(1+1/a)}), and the
  continuous-mixture boundary (modulus 1/log n, outside the theory).
